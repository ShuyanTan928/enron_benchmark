  1. the hidden fact — the same thing that is actually true (above);
  2. the direction — that the victim is LED TO BELIEVE the opposite of this fact (what is actually true

  3. the actor — the same person keeping it from them; and

is actually true with what the victim is led to believe), names the wrong actor or wrong victim (or
omits who is hiding it from whom), or only says something generic like "someone is hiding something".
